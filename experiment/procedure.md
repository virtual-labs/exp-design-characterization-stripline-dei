In the "Concept" tab, read the basic theory of stripline and its comparison with microstrip.
Click the "Formulas" tab to note down the exact TEM formulas used for stripline design.
Click the "Design & Simulation" tab to open the design and simulation dashboard.
Enter the design parameters (strip width W, ground spacing b, permittivity εr, loss tangent tanδ, line length L, load resistance Re(ZL), load reactance Im(ZL)) in the Design Parameters panel.
Observe the live-calculated values updating automatically as you type in the parameters.
Enter the simulation frequency range (f_start, f_stop) and the number of points in the Simulation bar.
Click the "▶ S-Parameters" button to run the S-parameter simulation.
Observe the |S11| and |S21| (dB) graph, VSWR graph, and standing wave |V(z)| graph generated.
Observe the reflection coefficient locus on the Smith Chart.
Observe the equivalent circuit diagram along with its computed values.
Click the "⚡ Field Distribution" button to run the field simulation.
Move the frequency slider to observe the E-field, H-field, |V(z)|, and |I(z)| distribution at different frequencies.
Change one design parameter at a time and re-run the simulation to observe its effect on the characteristic impedance and S-parameters.
Record all observations and write a conclusion based on the results obtained.
